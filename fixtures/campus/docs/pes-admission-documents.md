# PES University Documents Required for Admission

Classification: public
Owner: PES University (public website)
Source: https://pes.edu/docs/
Version: Retrieved 17 October 2026 · Paraphrased summary for the TraceFence demo, not an official PES document.

## 1. Common rules
- Bring two sets of self-attested photocopies of every document.
- Six passport-size and six stamp-size colour photographs.
- Copies of Aadhaar and of PAN cards for the candidate and a parent (needed to open a bank account).
- Original 10th and 10+2 marks cards, transfer or leaving certificate, and the original tuition fee receipt.
- A migration certificate if the candidate did not study under the Karnataka PU Board.
- Foreign nationals: passport and visa copies (OCI card instead of visa for OCI candidates).

## 2. B.Tech
- KCET quota: CET admission order and fee receipts, plus reservation certificates if claimed.
- PESU Merit (JEE Main / KCET): rank card, application form copy and a cancelled cheque leaf.
- Management, NRI, OCI and sponsored seats: score card copy, cancelled cheque leaf, and an AIU eligibility certificate where 10+2 used grades.

## 3. Other programmes
- MBBS: KEA allotment order and fee receipt, NEET score card, fitness certificate, local guardian authorisation for hostel residents, and anti-ragging undertakings from student and parent.
- Lateral entry, MBA/MCA/M.Tech, Law, Pharmacy and PhD each add their own entrance or degree documents.
- Working LLB candidates add an appointment order and 6 months of salary statements.
