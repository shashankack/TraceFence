# PES University Anti-Ragging Policy

Classification: public
Owner: PES University (public website)
Source: https://pes.edu/antiragging/ · https://pes.edu/terms/ · https://pes.edu/faqs/
Version: Retrieved 17 October 2026 · Paraphrased summary for the TraceFence demo, not an official PES document.

## 1. Commitment
PES University aims to give every student a safe, inclusive and respectful campus. Ragging in any form is prohibited on campus, in hostels, in associated facilities and online.

## 2. Rules it follows
The policy follows the anti-ragging guidelines of the University Grants Commission (UGC) and, for medical programmes, the National Medical Commission (NMC).

## 3. Who enforces it
An anti-ragging committee headed by the Vice-Chancellor oversees enforcement.

## 4. Consequences
- Ragging is treated as serious misconduct and leads to disciplinary action.
- Students found involved can be expelled and handed over to the police.

## 5. Reporting
- Students can report incidents without fear of retaliation.
- At admission, students and parents sign anti-ragging undertakings (listed among required documents for some programmes).
