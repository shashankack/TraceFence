# PES University Research Policy

## 1. Principles
Academic freedom within ethics, transparency on criteria and promotion, safe and retrievable research data, interdisciplinary work, and shared equipment across the university.

## 2. Integrity and misconduct
- Researchers must be honest and accurate, follow approved protocols and protect confidential data.
- All publications carry the PES University affiliation.
- Suspected misconduct goes to the Dean of Research, then the Vice-Chancellor, whose decision is final.

## 3. Intellectual property
- Inventions made with university resources must be disclosed and are owned by the university.
- Students, visiting faculty and industry staff using university resources sign the IP and copyright agreement.
- A 10% administrative overhead and patent costs are deducted from royalties before the inventors' share.

## 4. Internal grants
| Scheme | Amount | Term |
|---|---|---|
| Initiating Research (new PhD faculty) | INR 2 lakh (+1 lakh possible) | 24 months |
| CoE Research Project | Up to INR 60 lakh | 3 years, +1 on review |

IR funds cannot buy laptops, printers or outside consultancy.

## 5. Scholarships and stipends
- PhD scholarship from INR 27,000 a month for up to 4 years, 10% yearly increment on milestones.
- Research assistants from INR 15,000 a month; junior research fellows INR 6,000 to 12,000.
- Holders of other scholarships must give up the university award.

## 6. PhD milestones
Coursework in year 1, proposal defence in year 2, an indexed publication from year 2, and before submission two journal papers or one paper plus a patent.

## 7. Conflicts of interest
Any real or perceived conflict must be disclosed to the Dean of Research.
