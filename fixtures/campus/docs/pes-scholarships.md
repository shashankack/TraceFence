# PES University Scholarships

Classification: public
Owner: PES University (public website)
Source: https://pes.edu/scholarships/
Version: Retrieved 17 October 2026 · Paraphrased summary for the TraceFence demo, not an official PES document.

## 1. Merit scholarships
| Award | Who qualifies | Benefit |
|---|---|---|
| Top 5% | Top 5% of students by CGPA | Merit certificate and 50% tuition fee refund |
| Next 20% | Next 20% of students by SGPA | Merit certificate and 40% tuition fee refund |
| Distinction | SGPA of 7.75 or higher | Merit certificate |

## 2. Other support
- Corporate scholarships for final-year students, funded by partner companies.
- Teaching assistantships and research assistantships, both paid.

## 3. Conditions
- Only performance after joining the university counts.
- Final-year students are not eligible for merit scholarships or distinction awards.
- One award per semester or year.
- The Scholarship Committee sets any extra conditions each year, and the university may change the scheme.
- No scholarships are offered before admission.
