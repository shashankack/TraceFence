# PES University Online Application Terms, Privacy and Refunds

Classification: public
Owner: PES University (public website)
Source: https://pes.edu/terms/
Version: Retrieved 17 October 2026 · Paraphrased summary for the TraceFence demo, not an official PES document.

## 1. Using the application site
- Registering and accepting the terms creates a binding agreement for transactions on the site.
- Register only once. Applicants must be at least 18 or legally able to agree.
- Keep your password safe; you are responsible for transactions made with it.

## 2. Applications and payment
- An application is confirmed only after the form is complete and the fee is paid; a Transaction ID is then issued.
- Cards and net banking are accepted through payment gateways.
- A duplicate charge is refunded. Paying the processing fee repeatedly may lead to deregistration.

## 3. Privacy
PES collects name, phone number, email, state and country to process registrations, run services and improve the site, and discloses information only when legally required.

## 4. Refunds and cancellations
- Entrance test application fee: no refund once the transaction succeeds.
- Admission processing fee: non-refundable.
- Programme course fee: refunded according to current UGC refund norms.

## 5. Liability
PES does not guarantee uninterrupted service and is not liable for wrong details entered by the applicant or events beyond its control.
