# PES University Internal Committee (Prevention of Sexual Harassment)

Classification: public
Owner: PES University (public website)
Source: https://pes.edu/internal-committee/
Version: Retrieved 17 October 2026 · Paraphrased summary for the TraceFence demo, not an official PES document.

## 1. Purpose
The Internal Committee handles complaints of sexual harassment at the workplace and on campus, following the Supreme Court's Vishaka guidelines (1997) and related law.

## 2. Definition
Sexual harassment means any unwelcome gesture, behaviour, words or advances that are sexual in nature.

## 3. Who is covered
Women employees and any woman entering the workplace as a client, apprentice, daily-wage or ad-hoc worker, as well as students, research scholars and hospital patients.

## 4. What the institution must do
- Include a rule against sexual harassment in the code of conduct.
- Run a committee headed by a woman.
- Take disciplinary action against offenders and protect the complainant's interests.
- Make women staff and students aware of their rights.

## 5. Status of the committee
Following the Medha Kotwal Lele judgment, the committee acts as an inquiry authority and its report is treated as an inquiry report under the Central Civil Services (Conduct) Rules, 1964.

## 6. Further documents
The site links the PESU Code on Prevention of Sexual Harassment, the UGC Regulations 2015 and the PESU ICC guidelines.
