# PES University Student Grievance Redressal

Classification: public
Owner: PES University (public website)
Source: https://pes.edu/grc/ · https://support.pes.edu/grievance/
Version: Retrieved 17 October 2026 · Paraphrased summary for the TraceFence demo, not an official PES document.

## 1. Purpose
The Grievance Redressal Committee (GRC) was set up under UGC regulations so students can raise concerns openly, without fear of retaliation, and get quick resolution and counselling.

## 2. What can be raised
- Discrimination, exclusion or preference based on caste, creed, religion, language, ethnicity, gender or disability.
- Everyday campus concerns: facilities, canteen, transport, sports, hostel, IT and Wi-Fi, library and parking.

## 3. How it works
1. File a complaint through the online Student Grievance form.
2. A counsellor also opens the campus suggestion box regularly and forwards responses to the Vice-Chancellor, Registrar and GRC.
3. The committee reviews complaints following the principles of natural justice.
4. It reports to the Vice-Chancellor with recommendations.
5. Action-taken reports are tracked at later meetings.

## 4. Regulations
UGC (Redress of Grievances of Students) Regulations, 2019. The public self-disclosure also lists a Student Grievance Redressal Committee and an Ombudsperson.
