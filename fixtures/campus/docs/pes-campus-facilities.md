# PES University Campus Facilities and Student Support

Classification: public
Owner: PES University (public website)
Source: https://pes.edu/faqs/ · https://support.pes.edu/pesu/general/ · https://pes.edu/transportation/
Version: Retrieved 17 October 2026 · Paraphrased summary for the TraceFence demo, not an official PES document.

## 1. Clubs and events
About 50 clubs cover music, dance, culture, technology and social impact. The annual techno-cultural fest is Atmatrisha.

## 2. Sports
Cricket ground, indoor and outdoor basketball courts, table tennis, mini hockey pitch, gym, jogging track, yoga studio and physiotherapy centre.

## 3. Food
Two canteens serve North and South Indian food, plus snack and coffee points.

## 4. Health and counselling
- An infirmary with a full-time doctor, an ambulance and a tie-up with a specialty hospital.
- Psychiatric and career counselling with full-time professionals.

## 5. Library and Wi-Fi
- A central library and departmental libraries with e-access to journals and databases.
- Wi-Fi in all buildings, not in open areas.

## 6. Transport
College buses connect the city to the Ring Road and Electronic City campuses; route details are on each campus page.

## 7. What is not provided
No laptops or freebies, and no university-arranged off-campus housing.
