# Hostel Rules

Classification: public
Owner: Hostel Warden Office (synthetic)
Version: AY 2026–27 · Demo corpus only

NullTrace Institute residences: North House, South House, and Graduate Annex. These rules apply to all residents.

## Curfew
Weekday hostel curfew is **10:00 PM** (Monday to Thursday). Weekend curfew is **11:30 PM** (Friday to Sunday).

## Exceptions
- Late entry after curfew requires a late pass approved by the hostel warden **before 6:00 PM** on the same day.
- Library study nights during exam weeks extend curfew to **midnight** for residents who sign the exam-week register at the hostel desk.
- Medical emergencies never require prior approval; inform the hostel desk on return and file a note within 24 hours.

## Guests
Day guests must sign in at the hostel desk with a photo ID. Overnight guests are not permitted without prior written approval from the warden. Guests leave by 20:00 on weekdays and 21:00 on weekends.

## Quiet hours
Quiet hours run from **23:00 to 07:00** every day. Music and calls after quiet hours must use headphones.

## Mess & dining
| Meal | Weekday | Weekend |
|---|---|---|
| Breakfast | 07:30 – 09:30 | 08:00 – 10:00 |
| Lunch | 12:00 – 14:00 | 12:30 – 14:30 |
| Dinner | 19:00 – 21:00 | 19:00 – 21:00 |

Special diets: register with mess@nulltrace.example at least 5 working days ahead.

## Room & safety
- No open flames, induction cooktops, or personal heaters without written approval.
- Fire drills are mandatory; missing two drills in a term triggers a warden meeting.
- Room changes are frozen after week 3 except for documented welfare reasons.
