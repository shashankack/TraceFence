# Hostel Rules

Classification: public

## Curfew
Weekday hostel curfew is **10:00 PM** (Monday to Thursday). Weekend curfew is **11:30 PM** (Friday to Sunday).

## Exceptions
Late entry after curfew requires a late pass approved by the hostel warden before 6:00 PM on the same day.
Library study nights during exam weeks extend curfew to midnight for residents who sign the exam-week register.
Medical emergencies never require prior approval; inform the hostel desk on return.

## Guests
Day guests must sign in at the hostel desk. Overnight guests are not permitted without prior written approval.

## Quiet hours
Quiet hours run from 11:00 PM to 7:00 AM every day.
