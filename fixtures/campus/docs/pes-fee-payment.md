# PES University Fee Payment Policy

Classification: public
Owner: PES University (public website)
Source: https://pes.edu/fee/ · https://pes.edu/faqs/
Version: Retrieved 17 October 2026 · Paraphrased summary for the TraceFence demo, not an official PES document.

## 1. Payment mode
Fees are accepted only by demand draft (DD) payable at Bengaluru. Cash, cheque and bank transfers are not accepted on the fee page.

## 2. Full payment
Fees must be paid in full. Instalments are not available.

## 3. Charges
- Most programmes carry a one-time, non-refundable administrative charge of INR 10,000.
- Management, NRI, foreign, sponsored, OCI and PIO seats have separate fees; contact the Office of Admissions.

## 4. Financial help
- Loyalty concessions and PESU scholarships are listed separately.
- Education loans of up to 100% of fees are available through a partner bank.

## 5. Deadlines
Payment deadlines are published on the Important Dates page.
