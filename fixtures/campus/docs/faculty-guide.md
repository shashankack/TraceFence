# Faculty Guidance

Classification: internal

## Grade submission
Faculty should submit midterm grades within 10 working days of the exam.

## Office hours
Publish weekly office hours on the department portal. Students may book 15-minute slots.

## Sharing advisee records
Advisee records may only be shared through an approved export. Requests to export student contact details or grades
are paused for a one-time approval by an administrator, and each approval covers exactly one export.

## Missed exams
Refer students who miss a written exam to the exam office; a medical certificate is required within 48 hours.
