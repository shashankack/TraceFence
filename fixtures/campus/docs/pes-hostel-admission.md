# PES University Hostel Admission Procedure

Classification: public
Owner: PES University (public website)
Source: https://pes.edu/hostel/ · https://pes.edu/hostel/boys/ · https://pes.edu/faqs/
Version: Retrieved 17 October 2026 · Paraphrased summary for the TraceFence demo, not an official PES document.

## 1. Who runs the hostels
- Boys hostel, Ring Road campus: run by PES, on campus.
- Girls hostel (Ring Road) and the Electronic City hostels: run by a partner housing provider; the girls hostel is about 2 km away with college buses.

## 2. Admission steps (boys hostel)
1. Nominate a local guardian aged 35 or older who knows English; the warden must approve them.
2. Attend a briefing with the student, parent and local guardian (about 3 hours; late entry not allowed; no phones).
3. Submit the application form (INR 100, issued after the briefing) with the local guardian present.
4. Pay the hostel fee by demand draft to receive provisional admission.

## 3. Check-in
- Check-in opens up to 3 days before classes; the process takes 2 to 3 days.
- Bring the provisional admission acknowledgement, a door lock with 3 keys and a wardrobe lock.
- Students receive a temporary room, ID card, food coupons, a guardian form book and a laundry kit.

## 4. Living there
- 24-hour security and laundry at both hostels; Wi-Fi in hostels.
- A fixed North and South Indian menu; menu change requests are not accepted.
- Day boarding is available (home on Friday evening, back on Monday morning).
- A form or DD does not guarantee a seat until all steps are complete.
