# NullTrace Institute — Student Handbook

Classification: public

Welcome to NullTrace Institute. This handbook covers campus life basics for all enrolled students.

## Library hours
The main library is open 8:00 AM – 10:00 PM on weekdays and 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM on weekends.

## ID cards
Carry your student ID at all times on campus. Lost cards can be replaced at Student Services.
