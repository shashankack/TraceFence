# PES Group Loyalty Programme (Fee Concessions)

Classification: public
Owner: PES University (public website)
Source: https://pes.edu/loyalty/
Version: Retrieved 17 October 2026 · Paraphrased summary for the TraceFence demo, not an official PES document.

## 1. What it is
Annual fee concessions for employees' children, alumni and their families, for admissions from AY 2025-26 across PES Group institutions.

## 2. Concessions
| Category | Concession | Main condition |
|---|---|---|
| Children of current employees | 30% every year | Parent on full-time payroll for at least 5 years |
| PES Group alumni | 20% (10% in some cases) | Studied the required years at a PES institution |
| Siblings of students or alumni | 10% every year | Sibling completed at least 3 years at PES Group |
| Children of alumni | 10% every year | Parent studied at PES Group for at least 3 years |

## 3. Conditions
- Only one loyalty benefit can be claimed.
- It can be combined with other scholarships and assistantships.
- Not valid for government CET/PGCET quotas or for MD/MS programmes.
- It does not affect admission; the normal process still applies.
- The benefit ends if the employee leaves PES Group, and PES may change or withdraw the programme.

## 4. How to claim
Pay the tuition fee minus the concession by demand draft, submit the loyalty application form with documents, and expect later verification. Wrong claims must be repaid.
