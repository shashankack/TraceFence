# PES University Academic Rules (Timings, Attendance, Grading)

Classification: public
Owner: PES University (public website)
Source: https://pes.edu/faqs/ · https://support.pes.edu/pesu/general/
Version: Retrieved 17 October 2026 · Paraphrased summary for the TraceFence demo, not an official PES document.

## 1. Class timings
Six class hours run from 8:15 AM to 3:30 PM, with a short break at 10:15 AM and lunch from 12:45 PM to 1:30 PM.

## 2. Attendance
Students need at least **80% attendance** to sit university exams.

## 3. Grading
A 10-point grading system using both relative and absolute grading.

## 4. Academic support
- Faculty run mentoring sessions for difficult subjects.
- Class recordings are available on the student portal.

## 5. Programme options
- B.Tech students can take a minor in a different specialisation alongside their major.
- Integrated B.Tech-M.Tech can be chosen in the third year.
- Summer programmes are offered at partner universities abroad.

## 6. Dress code
There is no general dress code, except where a department requires one (for example Law, MBA and Hotel Management). Details are given at induction.
