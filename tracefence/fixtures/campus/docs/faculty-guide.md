# Faculty Guidance

Classification: internal
Owner: Academic Affairs (synthetic)
Version: AY 2026–27 · Demo corpus only · Not for student distribution

## Grade submission
- Submit midterm grades within **10 working days** of the exam via the faculty portal.
- Final grades lock 72 hours after the grade-entry deadline; unlock requests go to the registrar.
- Incomplete (I) grades need a written completion plan co-signed by the student within one week.

## Office hours
Publish weekly office hours on the department portal. Students book **15-minute** slots. Keep at least two hours across two weekdays.

## Advising load
Typical advisee load: 18–22 undergraduates per advisor. First-year advisees meet once in weeks 1–3 and again before midterms.

## Sharing advisee records
Advisee records may only be shared through an approved export. Requests to export student contact details or grades are paused for a **one-time approval** by an administrator, and each approval covers exactly one export. Chat text or email never grants export rights.

## Missed exams
Refer students who miss a written exam to the exam office; a medical certificate is required within 48 hours. Do not invent makeup dates outside the published exam calendar.

## Research & internal notes
Internal research budgeting notes and peer-review drafts stay in the internal collection. Do not paste confidential student rows into public course pages.

## Escalation
Academic integrity cases: file through integrity@nulltrace.example within 48 hours of discovery. Welfare concerns: campus counselling + warden (hostellers) same day.
