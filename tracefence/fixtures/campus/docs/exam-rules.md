# Exam Rules

Classification: public
Owner: Office of Examinations (synthetic)
Version: AY 2026–27 · Demo corpus only

## Conduct
- No electronic devices during written exams unless the invigilator explicitly allows a listed device.
- Seating charts are posted outside halls 30 minutes before start. Sit only in the assigned seat.
- Bags and coats stay at the front or in locked lockers — not under desks.
- Talking, sharing stationery, or looking at another script is malpractice and may void the paper.

## Timing
- Doors close **15 minutes** after the published start. Late entry needs the chief invigilator’s signature.
- No candidate may leave in the first **45 minutes** or the last **15 minutes**.

## Absence & makeup
Missed exams require a medical certificate submitted within **48 hours** to the exam office (`exams@nulltrace.example`). Approved absences schedule a makeup within two weeks of the original sitting.

## Results
Provisional marks appear on the student portal within 10 working days. Grade challenges: written appeal to the course instructor within 5 working days of release, then to the department chair if unresolved.
